Six programmes worth your time

The windows below are the usual pattern. Exact dates are published each year on the official site — that link is the one that matters.

GER DAAD, Germany

Opens June · closes mid-October (varies by country)

Mostly master's and PhD. Monthly stipend, health insurance and travel allowance. Separate tracks exist for development-related courses and for research grants, each with its own deadline.

Official scholarship database →

TUR Turkiye Burslari

Opens 10 January · closes 20 February

Bachelor's, master's and PhD. Covers tuition, dormitory, monthly stipend, health insurance, return flight and a one-year Turkish language course. One application covers several universities.

Official application calendar →

HUN Stipendium Hungaricum

Opens November · closes mid-January

Bachelor's, master's and PhD across more than 30 Hungarian universities with hundreds of English-taught programmes. Tuition, monthly stipend, housing allowance and insurance. Many countries require a nomination from their own government body first.

Official application page →

KOR Global Korea Scholarship

Undergraduate: September · Graduate: around February

Tuition, airfare, monthly allowance and a year of Korean language training. Two routes: through the Korean embassy in your country, or directly through a university. Quotas and documents differ between them.

Official Study in Korea page →

EU Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters

Most programmes: October to January

Two-year master's across several European universities, fully funded including travel and installation costs. There is no single deadline: every programme runs its own application, so you apply programme by programme.

Official Erasmus+ catalogue →

GBR Chevening, United Kingdom

Opens August · closes early November

One-year master's in the UK. Requires work experience, and you apply to universities separately from the scholarship itself.

Official Chevening page →
Before you build a plan around any date on this page: open the official link and confirm it. Deadlines shift by weeks between years, and some programmes need a nomination from your own country months before the scholarship itself closes.

Tuition-free countries, no scholarship needed

Public universities where international students pay no tuition or very little. You still cover living costs, and you must prove you can afford them to get a visa.

  • Germany, public universities
  • Austria, low fees
  • Czechia, free in Czech
  • Hungary, low fees
  • Poland, low fees

Germany is the exception people miss: Baden-Wurttemberg charges non-EU students tuition, so "free everywhere" is not accurate. Check the state and the university.
